# World Exploration and Mood

## World promise
A vibrant fantasy world with supernatural residents. The same world can be sunny, whimsical, rainy, mysterious or occasionally dangerous.

## Mood palette
- **Cozy:** meadows, streams, gardens, fishing spots, peaceful paths.
- **Whimsical:** luminous flora, friendly spirits, strange but charming creatures.
- **Lively:** the welcoming tavern and its guests.
- **Mysterious:** twilight groves, ancient ruins and hidden trails.
- **Eerie/dangerous:** selected cursed places and optional threats.

No universal fog, purple filter, perpetual darkness or horror-first styling.

## World structure
Start with a hand-built compact forest directly connected to the tavern. Expand with regions that introduce distinct ingredients, traversal opportunities and new guest requests. Prefer handcrafted density to huge empty maps.

## Vertical slice
One small forest, clear return route, five gatherable ingredient types total across the slice, and at least one discovery or environmental surprise. No mandatory combat.

## Perspective
2D top-down three-quarter, rectangular tiles, Y-sorted actors/props, believable occlusion and carefully simulated height. Not isometric.
