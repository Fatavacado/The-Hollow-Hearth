# Tavern Management

## Core promise
The tavern is the player's welcoming home and chosen business activity, **not a compulsory daily shift**.

## First slice
One room, simple cooking station, explicit Open/Closed state, two supernatural guest archetypes, readable orders, simple serving and coin rewards, one purchasable upgrade (likely satchel, which connects tavern earnings to exploration).

## Future tavern improvements
Kitchen stations and recipe capability; seating and service capacity; decorations and atmosphere; storage; optional conveniences. Each feature must add a decision or visible improvement rather than a spreadsheet layer.

## Service behavior
No guests spawn while closed. Opening signals availability; closing should be predictable and not trap the player in an endless queue. Missing ingredients should be visible before committing to an order. Guests are personable rather than uniformly frightening.

## Anti-scope rules
No staff AI, table reservation system, dynamic pricing simulator, complex reputation economy or full construction grid in the first slice.
