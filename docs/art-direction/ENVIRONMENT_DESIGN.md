# Copperfern main-region environment study

Status: bounded concept proposal within the owner-selected Copperfern main-region direction. No production tiles, collisions, navigation, gameplay or project settings changed.

## Forest and home exterior

A compact hand-built forest connects directly to the tavern, following WORLD_EXPLORATION.md and VERTICAL_SLICE.md. Favor clear return routes and purposeful discoveries over a huge map. Stepped conifer/fern masses frame quiet paths, with warm horizontal timber and restrained copper details linking architecture to the interior. Sunny ground and readable materials are essential; mystery is occasional contrast.

The composition studies a tavern at the upper right, a broad approach from lower center, small side gathering pockets and a stream at the edge. Stream/bridge are visual possibilities, not an approved traversal feature. Five gathering-cluster silhouettes are placeholders for the slice's five actual ingredient definitions, not new item names or mechanic commitments. Replace them with the existing game's definitions when the slice is available. A cutaway roof may be needed in gameplay; the exterior concept is not a validated occlusion solution.

## Tavern interior

One room with a stove and prep area on the left, serving counter to the rear, seating on the right and generous circulation from the entrance. Keep hands, carried bowls, guests, station approach areas and order indicators visible. Warmth comes from timber and localized hearth light, with cool shadows preserving material contrast. The room supports voluntary service; decorations must not imply compulsory hours or new management systems.

Coal-spirit and mushroom-scholar silhouettes are guest appearance candidates only. Final guest types and behavior come from the game plan and actual slice.

## Common construction

Use rectangular tile planes, non-isometric top-down three-quarter view, consistent top/front faces and stepped/bracket motifs. Copper is a selective material accent, not a global tint. Keep ground quiet and ingredient silhouettes distinct using shape and value. Preserve a single game-wide player scale and density so future regions can change ecology and materials without changing rendering style.

## Translation to production

After layout review, author one small terrain transition family, tree/trunk/canopy layers, tavern facade modules and modular furniture at the validated project scale. Candidate 32×32 cells remain provisional. A concept illustration is not a seamless TileSet atlas. Validate terrain peering combinations, repeated patterns, floor edges, door/actor scale and collision footprints in Godot. Separate canopies/roofs from foot-level obstacles, then test Y-sorting and occlusion while walking all directions. Test station approach and serving routes with the actual player, not an open empty room. Do not create an entire biome library before this benchmark passes.
