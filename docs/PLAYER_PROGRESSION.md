# Player Progression — Design Targets

Three linked but separate tracks:
1. **Player experience:** gain XP through exploration, gathering, cooking and serving. Level-ups grant modest, meaningful choices, not endless stat inflation.
2. **Tools and equipment:** satchel, harvesting implements and later region-specific gear. Upgrades should unlock a new action, reduce friction or enable new discoveries.
3. **Discovery:** recipes, region access and knowledge gained through exploration and guest encounters.

## First slice
One upgrade only: **Expanded Satchel** (provisional). It should increase carrying capacity and be demonstrably useful on the next trip. The slice may track XP without implementing a full skill tree; do not fake a complete level system.

## Future examples, not commitments
Better gathering tools, fishing rod, garden tools, cooking proficiency, safer traversal, recipe mastery and specialized exploration perks.

## Balancing constraints
No grind-only level gates. Don't reward repetitive spam more than exploration and service. Make upgrade costs attainable in a short slice. Ensure player cannot permanently lose the ability to earn.
